## Not run, and why

* A full `make cm-ui-gate` or `ui-cm-release-evidence-gate` (about 70 minutes): not rerun after the last Contract Management edit. I ran the two Contract Management specs that cover this change (`cm-existing-contract.spec.ts` and `cm-funding.spec.ts`) in one world. The gate in the Makefile now lists `cm-funding.spec.ts` after `cm-existing-contract.spec.ts`.
* `npx vitest run --project home` (part of `cm-regression-gate`): not run. Nothing I changed reaches Home. The Award board suite (57 vitest) did run.
* Contract Management's own formation of the canonical award: still blocked by FU-CM-70 (Form No. 4 declares no insertions) and FU-CM-72 (the entity's principal address is not held). The canonical-award journey therefore stands in for Contract Management and for the interim reservation authority; the independent-contract journey needs neither.
* The Playwright global teardown (`tests/ui/globalTeardown.ts`, through the Bid Submission `restore_site`) puts the stored test clock back to unset after ANY browser run, while the canonical validation expects it at the anchor. The seed session changed the teardown (uncommitted at the time of writing) to put the clock back on the anchor as its last step; that change is theirs and is not part of this build.
* A third financial year: the canonical world has two Active years after the closed one, so the three-year example of the specification was run for two years.
* Mutation checks of the guards (segregation, rows mismatch, the verification and stale-source checks, atomic rollback): not run.
* The error-banner, closed-year and stale-revision variants of the correction screens, and the error state of the funding screens: not walked in a browser.
* The development site: nothing was run there by me.
* `make seed-canonical-validate` on the test site fails at the time of writing for reasons that are not this build's: Planning test rows (units, fiscal years 2101-2102 and 2103-2104, `reqt.*` and `plnt.*` users, budget BUD-PLNT-0001, one reservation and four Budget audit events of Requisitions, made at 11:59 and 13:03 on 17 October) and the site test clock, which was unset before my first run (the Contract Management world recorded an empty previous instant each time and restored it as such). My own runs leave no `bud.*` users, no isolated budgets, no "KT Budget Test" units and no funding requests on the canonical Budget.
