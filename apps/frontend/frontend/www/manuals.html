Choose a guide for setting up a supplier account or creating a Procuring Entity workspace.

Set up a supplier account

Register the organisation that will prepare and submit bids.

Start supplier registration
  1. 1

    Sign in

    Supplier account registration requires a signed-in user. If you are signed out, sign in first; you will be returned to the registration form.

  2. 2

    Enter the organisation’s legal details

    Provide the legal name, country, registration number, KRA PIN, registered address, and official contact details.

  3. 3

    Confirm your authority

    Enter your job title as the Authorised Signatory and upload evidence of your authority. The form accepts PDF, PNG, or JPG/JPEG files.

  4. 4

    Create the account and verify email

    Submit the form. A verification link is sent to the organisation’s official email. Open that link to verify the contact and continue.

Before you begin

Have your organisation’s registration details and signatory-authority evidence ready.

Account setup gives your organisation access to bid preparation. It does not approve or prequalify the organisation for a tender.

Create a Procuring Entity workspace

Set up the organisation workspace and verify its administrator’s contact details.

Register a Procuring Entity
  1. 1

    Enter account and organisation details

    Provide the organisation name, a unique workspace name, administrator name and email, country, contact phone, and a password.

  2. 2

    Choose optional workspace settings

    Set the industry, organisation size, financial-year start, and default currency if applicable.

  3. 3

    Verify the administrator’s email

    Request the email code, enter it on the verification step, and continue after the address is confirmed.

  4. 4

    Verify the contact phone and submit

    Request the SMS code, verify the phone, accept the terms, then submit the workspace request. The completion screen displays the workspace status and URL when available.

Choose a workspace name carefully

The workspace name becomes part of the site address. Use lowercase letters, numbers, and single hyphens.

Keep access to the administrator email and phone available throughout registration; changing either after verification resets the verification steps.
