**Accepted live interaction, 27 September 2026.** AO appoints the committee and publishes attendance arrangements. The appointed members join individually and the recorder records actual supplier/representative attendance. After BDS closes, the chair begins with the full roster and opens each current timely bid in sequence. A member reads the exact statutory facts aloud; only after the actual announcement does the chair/recorder commit the readout row. An attendee may request a repeat or raise a procedural observation; the chair handles it, and the recorder records the speaker, affected bid, factual observation, response and time. A member's differing account is retained. Material disconnection or verification failure pauses the next reveal. At close, the register and actual session evidence populate draft minutes; each member reviews and attests their own exact targets. ## 2. Purpose, outcomes and scope

The result is an auditable opening in which every current timely package is accounted for; each tender is numbered, its pages counted, and statutory facts read and registered as submitted; the committee, attendees, actual procedure and interruptions are documented; required member attestations bind exact material; and Evaluation receives unchanged opened packages with the published mappings.

This document covers appointment, sealed-box verification, opening access, ceremony, attendance, one-by-one readout, register, minutes, exceptions, lawful disclosure and handoff. It excludes bid submission, evaluation and scoring, qualification, arithmetic correction, bidder clarification, award, contract formation, paper-bid intake, and later separate financial-envelope openings.

**Committee-presence invariant:** appointment alone is insufficient. The custody service also enforces independent multi-person opening control at the decrypt/reveal boundary whenever an envelope exists; the application check alone is insufficient. If a member leaves, pause before the next material action. They may return and re-authenticate; if unavailable, the AO must make a lawful, audited successor appointment and the replacement must participate from the point of resumption. Earlier events retain their actual participating roster and times. No retrospective substitution, proxy presence or reduced-quorum override. The legal profile must validate this conservative full-roster rule and any exceptional continuation before production.

Presence is an authenticated, server-maintained ceremony session with an explicit join and recorded departure/disconnection, not a checkbox set by the chair or a passive name in the appointment. The approved custody design may keep members' independent authorizations valid for the live session; it need not demand three repetitive clicks for every bid. It must invalidate an absent member's authority promptly and deny the next reveal. This exact mechanism and timeout are operating-profile inputs, not a UI assumption.

The submitted total read aloud is fixed and never corrected at opening. For this product BDS permits no alternative price or unrequested discount. Any future permitted modification needs a published mapping and its own readout rule. Act section 82 also protects the submitted sum read aloud from later adjustment, subject to its stated exceptions.

**State map.** BOP Awaiting deadline/Ready to open ↔ PRC Pending, including member arrival and custody participation labelled **pre-session**, without a PRC start time; BOP Opening/Interrupted ↔ PRC In session; BOP Readout complete, including its empty-outcome branch, ↔ PRC Session ended; BOP Awaiting attestations ↔ PRC Awaiting attestations; BOP Opening complete (nonempty or no bids) ↔ PRC Finalized; only nonempty completion emits an Evaluation handoff. PRC Not held is terminal and applies only where BOP never began; PRC Aborted after start is terminal on TPR cancellation. Neither creates successful opening or Evaluation handoff.

**Roster and target scope.** Each segment retains actual roster, appointment, time and entry IDs. A successor never attests another member’s participation or acts. The owner enumerates each actual member’s targets by segment. An unavailable departed member’s required proof stays blocked until a legally approved alternative procedure is specified; a successor or administrator cannot substitute or backdate it.

**Product vocabulary.** In Bid Opening, `PRC_VERSION_CONFLICT` maps to `BOP_VERSION_CONFLICT` and its exact §8 message; no second conflict message is displayed. `PRC_ALREADY_FINALIZED` uses **This opening record is final. Add a correction if a fact needs to change.** and the §10.6 route. The owner maps PRC errors to plain production copy and the approved signing/custody method. No verification label or proxy action is a production screen. The Trust method remains a release gate; a designer must not infer a signature control from a simulated proof.
