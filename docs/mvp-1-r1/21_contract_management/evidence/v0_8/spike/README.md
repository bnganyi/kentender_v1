## S5 in detail (the highest-risk item)

1. **Native maker/approver works through the workflow** (`s5a`): a Payment Entry workflow with a separate approver role and `allow_self_approval = 0` — the maker cannot approve ("Not a valid Workflow Action"), a separate approver can, and a user holding both roles approving their own entry is refused ("Self approval is not allowed").
2. **The workflow alone is bypassable** (`s5a`): a user with native submit rights called `submit()` on a payment still in Draft and it posted straight to "Approved"; Administrator likewise; the Desk-save submit path likewise. Native rights are insufficient (CM §5.12).
3. **Native hold is not a payment hold** (`s5b_…_native_hold`): against an invoice with `on_hold = 1`, the direct and API entries posted with **no allocation** (ERPNext simply left the held invoice off the suggested references), so the money left as an unallocated payment to the supplier; the Journal Entry settled the held invoice completely (status Paid); Payment Reconciliation offered no allocation for the held invoice ("No records found in Allocation table"), so that one route was stopped natively. The native check only fires when a reference row exists.
4. **Every route settles an uncertified invoice natively** (`s5b_…_uncertified`): direct entry, API entry, Journal Entry, and an advance reconciled by Payment Reconciliation all took the invoice to Paid. Each fires a hookable event: `before_submit` (Payment Entry, Journal Entry) and `before_update_after_submit` (Payment Entry, on reconciliation).
5. **One guard on those events closes every route** (`s5c`, guard in `guards.py`): outside the controlled operation all four routes, direct `submit()` by a dual-role user, by Administrator, the Desk-save submit, the workflow Approve button and a background job (`s5f`) are refused; the invoice is untouched and nothing posts. Inside the operation: 500,000 + 300,000 settle the 800,000 invoice; against a 600,000 certification the next 200,000 is refused ("certified for 600,000.00, already settled 500,000.00"); own-entry approval is refused even inside the operation.
6. **A defect in my first guard was found by the test**, not by reading: it measured "new allocations" against the saved draft, so the entitlement check did nothing at submission. Corrected (`guards._new_allocations`) and re-run.
7. **Changed amount or beneficiary invalidates a pending approval** (`s5c`, `s5d`): an edit to the amount, allocation or bank account of an entry awaiting approval returns it to Draft; approving without resubmission is refused.
8. **Concurrency needs a row lock** (`s5e`): two approvers paying 500,000 each against an 800,000 certification from separate processes — **without the lock both posted (1,000,000 cash) and the invoice showed only 500,000 settled** (a lost update, books and cash disagree); **with the lock exactly one posted** and the other was refused.
9. **Limits**: a write that skips the controller (`frappe.db.sql`, `db_set`, direct database access) is outside any document-event guard, as core's command-write guard documents; CM v0.8 §6 already declines to claim otherwise. Data Import was **source-read** (`importer.py` calls `doc.insert()` and `doc.submit()`, the same controller path), not executed. Allocating a credit note to an invoice through Payment Reconciliation creates ledger entries without a Payment or Journal Entry document event; it moves no cash and was not executed.

## S7 in detail (reversal order, from real documents)

- Cancelling a posted receipt is refused while its Quality Inspection exists (`LinkExistsError`) and after it has been invoiced ("Purchase Invoice … is already submitted"); the order is **inspection → receipt**, or **invoice → receipt**. In the right order the receipt reverses (live stock entries 0, ledger rows reversed, order back to 0% received); Serial No records remain.
- With a submitted asset the receipt cannot be cancelled until the asset is cancelled; then it can (assets left 0).
- A unit returned after payment: stock return −1 (serial status "Delivered"), debit note −100,000 (outstanding −100,000, original 0), ledger balance +100,000 (supplier owes), and **no refund payment is created automatically**.

## S8 in detail

- The installed release stores `02_master/complete_tender.html` as a private file with its SHA-256; read through the database, it recomputes to the recorded digest (202,976 bytes).
- **Form No. 4** is found by its heading text, not by an id: the master has 25 element ids and none for Form No. 4, the GCC or the SCC. The fragment (3,170 bytes) has 15 blanks, 4 template placeholders, the 8 incorporated documents a)–h) and 2 witness lines. A missing heading fails visibly.
- **Typed contract values exist in the release** (`insertion_points.csv`): payment terms and days, performance security (required, percentage, form), delay damages (rate per week, cap), acceptance office, contact office. None exist for advance, invoice process, change, termination or warranty discharge.
- **Generation is byte-reproducible**: the same inputs give the same HTML SHA-256 and the same PDF SHA-256 through the existing wkhtmltopdf path, within a process and across separate processes.
- The official PPRA DOCX (hash `ee081f60…f81`, as in CM §19.2) was downloaded and the paragraph window 2647–2667 reproduced: it is Form No. 4. The release's text matches it at 94.5% normalised similarity (a typo fixed, a lettered list, an instruction line, a different signing-block layout). The **issued release governs**, so this is informational.

## What this means for the build (carry into Phase 3 and later)

**Configuration the build depends on** (each is a site-wide or master-data change to be owned by CFG / Finance, not a per-delivery user choice):
1. Custom fields: `cm_operation_identity` on Purchase Order, Purchase Receipt, Purchase Invoice and Payment Entry (`no_copy`, indexed); `cm_certified_amount` on Purchase Invoice; `cm_unit_reference` on Asset.
2. Stock Settings: **Enable Serial and Batch No for Item** (required for serial items to post; global).
3. Accounts Settings: **Check supplier invoice uniqueness** (global; CM also keeps its own duplicate rule).
4. A Mode of Payment account and a bank account for the company; an Asset Category and items with `auto_create_assets`, an asset naming series and a location on each receipt row; Quality Inspection Parameter masters (one per frozen contract criterion).
5. A Payment Entry workflow (and, as chosen, Purchase Invoice) with a distinct approver role and `allow_self_approval = 0`; the mapping from AUTH responsibilities (Finance preparation, Finance approval) to ERPNext roles.
6. `bill_for_rejected_quantity_in_purchase_invoice` is **on** by default; rejected units never reach a receipt in this design, so it is moot, but the spec's "do not permit billing rejected quantities" argues for turning it off.

**Integration code** (Phase 3 unless noted): the guard of `guards.py` promoted to `services/`, with the row lock, the controlled-operation window, the supplier-level rule and the invalidation rule; the native adapter's identity lookup and link; serial refusal (no receipt row for a serial item with an unidentified unit); unit-reference assignment in the receipt's transaction; cancel/return ordering; net-payable read from the ledger; a **core signing facade with `reconcile(correlation_id)`** and an idempotent CM simulation provider; **owner-side providers** for Form No. 4, GCC/SCC and the schedules, with stable anchors (Phase 5).

**Not tested** (said plainly): a production signing provider; Data Import executed; Payment Reconciliation with Journal Entry vouchers and credit-note allocations; multiple companies and fiscal-year boundaries; Asset depreciation after submission; bank reconciliation and settlement evidence (CM-owned); MariaDB only for native behaviour (SQLite for independence only); a second ERPNext version.

**Cleanup (done 15 Oct 2026).** `world.teardown` and `native.remove_custom_fields` removed everything the spike created on the test site (documents, users, workflow, items, category, supplier, custom fields, the payment-mode account row); Stock Settings and Accounts Settings were restored by each scenario. **Left behind:** the GL account `CM Spike Bank - K` (cancelled ledger rows keep it referenced; no balance), and eleven far-future fiscal years (2849–3820) created by Budget's own race tests, which I ran for S7c and whose test budgets still link them — that suite's normal residue; one (`2752-2753`) was removable and removed.
