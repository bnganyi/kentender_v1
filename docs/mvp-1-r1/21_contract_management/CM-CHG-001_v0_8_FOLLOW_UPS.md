This register holds corrections owed to other documents, external decisions, evidence the requirements keep open, production gates, and items found while planning CM-CHG-001 v0.8. Rows are permanent; a closed row says how it closed. None blocks the module's own build unless its row says so, because CM v0.7 and v0.8 authorise incremental implementation (CM §17.3 gate 2; §19.9) while these remain open. They do block **package acceptance or production enablement of the behaviour they name**. **Exception (owner direction 14 Oct 2026, OD-E): FU-CM-05, 06, 07 and 32 are not deferred follow-ups. They are required Package B work (plan D20; tracker CM8-513…521): the owner-interface changes and the updates to the current documentation are implemented in Phase 5, and formation is not accepted until every required source is proved available.**

**Rules from the requirements, carried here:**
- Owner-document amendments are applied to **each owner's externally current version**, recording that version before editing; the earlier full-copy owner proposals are withdrawn (CM §17.10). Each amendment is written as a Proposed document under the document-change protocol, with a result per change: Applied / Already satisfied / Conflict or missing evidence.
- Open evidence and legal questions do not prevent authorised evidence work; they prevent acceptance and enablement of the affected behaviour (CM-AC-24).
- A legal question's answer changes a specific reviewed requirement or profile, never a runtime rule editor (CM §19.1).
