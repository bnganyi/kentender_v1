# **Phase 1: MVP – Core Procurement Processes**

## Wave 1

### Procurement Planning & Budgeting

- National development plans (short / medium /long-term)
- Procuring entities' plans (short / medium /long-term) \[corporate plans\]
- Budgeting (approval by board of directors, parliament, county assemblies)
  - Budget linking and validation
  - Budget locking after approval
- Departmental procurement plans
- Consolidate annual procurement plan (head of procurement)
- Approval of annual consolidated plan (Accounting Officer)

### Purchase Requisitions

- Department requisition, including technical and financial specifications creation
- System to check stock levels from stores
- Approval (head of department)
- Routing to Finance (to confirm budget approval validity)
- Routing to Accounting Officer (approval)
- Routing to head of procurement

## Wave 2

### Supplier Registration

- Specify methods
  - Request for quotation
  - Restricted tendering
  - Request for proposals
  - Direct procurement
- Approval by the head of procurement
- Supplier self-registration
- Published and publicized registration
- Use of another procuring entities' list of supplier per method
- Head of procurement registers from market knowledge
- Mandatory data capture (registration details,
  - Type: ltd company or company limited by guarantee, partnership, limited liability partnership, trusts, CBOs, business name, individuals,
  - registration number / ID for individuals
  - date of registration
  - registry
  - shareholding
  - Directorship / proprietorship
  - Type of supply (goods, works, services)
  - Market control (monopoly, oligopoly, …)
  - Annual trade / practices licenses
  - Annual permits
  - Product registration certificates (…)
  - Special permits (…)
  - Contact address (area, postal address, email address, phone, website, …)
  - Branches
  - Franchises / agents / subcontractors, authorized dealers
  - Auditor details
    - Verification of auditors details with ICPAK
- Ability to change / add supplier details (with audit trail)
- Verification of details after any change
- System to validate registration status – link to BRS, or dissolution by courts, or registrar of deaths for individuals
  - System to flag / terminate contract in this case
- Automatic validation of compliance documents
- Solvency – auditors certificate (digitally signed and stamped, date of issue and expiry)
- Professional / peer memberships (validate by system)
- Tax compliance status (TCC no, date of issue, expiry date)
- Conviction for corruption and abuse of office status (verification from judiciary)
  - Indicate case / investigation if any, institution (police, EACC, asset recovery, EMU, PPRA), status of case
  - Professional disciplinary proceedings

### Tender Document Preparation

- **_\[need to identify all methods of procurement\]_**
- System to automatically select the approved method when drafting the document
- Tender document creation (head of procurement)
- Route to user / technical department for verification and confirmation of completeness
- Provide interactive links to specification documents, e.g. technical drawings, sketches, etc.
- System to have pages where suppliers will see prepared tender documents (suppliers to declare interest)
- Route to accounting officer to approve publication and publicization
- Send to head of procurement to amend where necessary, publish and publicize
- System to select suppliers, with capability for additions / amendments

### Tender Submission & Opening

- Suppliers to fill in all tender forms (with ability to resume)
- Tenderer should be able to amend before submission deadline – system to allow amended version to be submitted
- Tenderer should be able to withdraw before submission deadline
- Confirmation of tender (time) submission to tenderer
- System to generate report indicating tenders received, total tender sum, tender security submitted (issuerer, date of issue, date of expiry, total security sum)
  - Sent to head of procurement, accounting officer, head of department, all tenderers that submitted tenders
- Tender closure controls

### Evaluation & Award

- Automated evaluation
  - Eligibility requirements examination (yes / no criteria)
  - Technical requirements compliance evaluation (pass /fail) \[weighted for RFP method\]
  - Financial requirements evaluation
  - Due diligence (lowest evaluated tenderer / highest score proposal (RFP))
- Evaluation report submitted to head of procurement to prepare professional opinion
- Professional opinion and evaluation report routed to accounting officer for adjudication and decision making
- Certificate of award or rejection or request for clarification issued by accounting officer
- Decision by accounting officer routed to head of procurement
- Head of procurement to respond to accounting officer in case of clarification
- Notification of outcome to all tenderers
  - Notification of award to successful tenderer (link to performance security form for the bank / insurance company, award acceptance template)
  - Notification of rejection to unsuccessful tenderers (notification to collect tender security)
- Negotiations with successful tenderer to agree on terms and conditions
- Submission of performance security
- Debriefing of unsuccessful tenderers
- Return of tender securities
- Appeals filed by unsuccessful tenderers to the PPARB (standstill period)

# **Phase 2: Contract Management**

## Wave 1

### Contract Execution

- Head of procurement prepares contract agreement form
- System to notify successful the tenderer to electronically sign the contract
- Procuring entity is notified of the signing. Accounting officer signs the contract electronically
- Contract goes to head of procurement to share a copy of the contract to the user department
- Head of procurement advises the accounting officer to appoint the contract implementation team
  - Works: CIT does site handover procedures, meetings (MoM), milestones
  - Goods: tests, milestones
- Head of procurement to recommend acceptance committee members (qualifications important)
- Upon completion of every milestone, CIT to have minutes indicating completion / delivery (together with supplier)
- Notice to head of procurement to notify inspection and acceptance committee to do tests after each milestone (user dept and accounting officer to be notified). Tests to be specified beforehand
- Minutes of inspection and acceptance committee with an interim certificate or penalties, GRNs for the committee to sign
- Certificates to go to head of procurement to share to CIT, supplier and accounting officer
- Supplier to submit invoice for delivered goods / services
- Collect data about CIT and Inspection and acceptance committee members. Allow secretary and non-member
- Complex goods / works should have a certificate of conformation by head of user department
- For non-technical, head of procurement to issue certificate of acceptance.
- Certificate package to be forwarded to user department head and accounting officer
- For final delivery, accounting officer to issue certificate of final acceptance
- System to pop up FAC for head of procurement to issue - triggers full payment
  - Sent to supplier to submit the final invoice
  - ETR linkage with KRA for statutory remittances
  - Invoice sent to head of procurement
  - Head of procurement forwards to head of finance
  - System to pop up payment voucher template for head of finance to verify
  - payment voucher to go to head of procurement to certify
  - voucher goes back to finance for GL processes (using supplied bank accounts)
- System to generate reports to show outstanding amounts for intermediate milestones; for final milestone to show amount less retentions
- Closeout upon completion
- Capability to send reminders for releasing retentions (head of procurement). Once period ends, system to automatically
  - Capability for deductions during retention period (with reasons)
- Head of procurement to prepare monthly contract monitoring report
- Claims template for filling by procuring entity; capability for system to calculate liquidated damages / penalties and send demand to contract (upon CIT meetings)
- Incentives template by the procuring entity
- Claims can be filed by contractor / supplier (interest, demand for payment)
- Dispute management (notices of claims, mediation, etc.).
  - Stop work orders by accounting officer (CIT, head of procurement advice)
- Payments (partial, final)
- Contract variations and amendments
- Termination of contract by accounting officer upon legal advice (CIT minutes, head of procurement recommendation).
  - Termination to have conditions (site / property handover, time to vacate site, site discharge notes)
  - Claims (penalties, refunds, settling of accounts, release of retention funds, etc.)
  - Notices to supplier / contractor before termination
- Contract close-out: FAC issued, goods handover to procuring entity (insurance kicks in, amortization, etc.)
- Managing the works or goods consumption
- Defect liability period to start from handover. Capability to reopen contract in case of defect

# **Phase 3: Financials & Asset Management**

## Wave 1

### IFMIS Integration (mainstream govt, county, etc.)

- Commitment control
- Payment status sync
- Budget consumption updates

## Wave 2

### Inventory control (planning)

- Valuation
- Inventory methods
- Tracking
- Optimal levels
- Inventory reports
- Reserving
- Receiving
  - Head of stores / warehouse holds the goods pending inspection and acceptance (conditional receipt). CIT should okay the goods with a report to certify delivery milestone.
  - Gives supplier acknowledgement of delivery note indicating subject to inspection and acceptance
  - Notice given to head of procurement indicating receipt of goods pending inspection
  - System to popup for head of procurement to recommend inspection team (should show expertise of people recommended, availability, and goods received in the warehouse). \[_Expertise to be collected prior\]_
  - This goes to the accounting officer for appointment. \[in case of rejection, provide a reason\]
  - Team does inspection (tests, etc.) and logs minutes / reports
  - System popups GRN /certificates for all to sign (should be after technical reports by inspection team) \[GRN is only issued by inspection and acceptance team\]
  - GRN routed to head of procurement
  - GRN also routed to user /technical department - Certificate of confirmation pops up to for signature (should see reports from inspection and acceptance team)
  - Routed to head of procurement who routes all reports (CIT reports, technical reports, GRN, CoC/CoA) to accounting officer.
  - System popups up certificate of final acceptance to the accounting officer to accept or reject with reasons
  - Certificate of final acceptance is routed to head of procurement
    - Notify CIT and IAC team
    - Notify supplier with certificate of final acceptance (for completed deliveries). Supplier can send invoice
    - Message to stores to take custody of goods
    - Message to Finance for costing and amortization
    - User notified that goods are ready for issuance
- SLAs for all the above activities with reminders
- Storing
  - Goods will be isolated till certificates are issued

### Stores Management

- Labour cost of all the above activities (to be allocated to the goods)
- System to pick final cost of the goods
- Depreciation
- Market value

### Requests and Issues

- Goods can only be issued on an approved request only
- Request goes to stores with a copy to head of procurement
- Stores issues to the requistioner
- System debits (FIFO / LIFO)
- Shelf life
  - System to indicate obsolesce, expiry
  - Block issues if goods are close to shelf-life threshold (should be indicated on CIT report, system to block receipt of those close to threshold). Good isolated for disposal.
  - System to warn if expiry period nears (parameterize)
  - Reports on obsolete / expired items and value
- Reports
  - Stock balances
  - reorder levels
  - warranties
  - general inventory reports
  - slow-moving goods (flag such items for reduction in reorder levels)
  - goods issued without requests / approval
  - damaged/lost goods reports
  - returned goods
  - defects (with values)
  - replacements under warranty

### Asset management

- Handover to operations and maintenance processes
- Assets register
  - Capital assets
  - Consumables
  - Movable / non-movable
- Assigned entity details
- Performance of the asset
- Service – maintenance scheduling and costs
- Book value
- Efficiency and effectiveness
- Yearly report and depreciated values
- General conditions of assets
- Obsolete / scrapped assets report
- Disposed items report (removed from asset register)
- Reports to feed into financial statements (assets / liabilities)

- Roles:
  - accounting officer
  - head of procurement
  - stores manager
- Asset tagging and tracking (serial numbers, RFID, air tags, qr codes, GPS)
- Efficient physical count processes
- Leased assets management
- Reverse logistics

### Asset Disposal

- Annual disposal plan
  - Item details
- Market survey
- Disposal committee appointed by the accounting officer
  - Verify the disposal recommendation
  - Consider who's using the items (data security) – \[guides disposal methods\]
  - Make recommendations to the accounting officer
- Disposal procedures
  - Notice of disposal (requisition to dispose)
  - Employee in charge of the asset / or person in technical dept initiates the disposal process
  - Notice is accompanied by technical report. Report guided by market survey
  - Valuation report done in lieu of technical report
  - Request approved by head of department before going to the disposal committee
  - Committee conducts survey, reviews items, and recommends to accounting officer the best method of disposal
  - Recommendation channeled to accounting officer through head of procurement
  - Accounting officer approves the request
  - Asset is removed from register upon approval with proper records
- Methods of disposal
  - Transfer to another procuring entity for value or no value
  - Sale by public tender
  - Sale by public auction (e-Auction option)
  - Trade-in
  - Waste disposal (various processes)
  - Disposal to employees

### Logistics

- Mode of transport selection
  - Select method of dispatch (handling of goods)
  - Select type of supplies (perishable, etc.)
  - Geography (origin and destination) (geopolitics, etc.)
  - Timing
  - Costing (air, sea, in-land, trans-shipment)
  - Safety measurements (fragile, high-value, perishable goods)
- Manage forwarding agents
- Packing of goods (depending on product type, environment, etc.)
  - Mode of transport
  - Route and destination (handling, safety)
  - climatic conditions
  - customs duties
  - cost of packaging
  - sustainable packaging
  - tracking of containers / LCL (Less-than-container load)
  - packing and shipping instructions
  - labeling and shipping marks (electronic labeling, **_research on this \[DHL\]_**)
- manage incoterms (international commercial terms) \[_EXW, FCA, FOB, CIF, DDP, DAP, etc._\]
  - identify validity
- Insurance during transportation, type of coverage (detailed)
- Shipping documents (electronic, preferably **_\[research\]_**)
- Receiving of goods (at the port)
- Restriction on import or export of goods (identifying counterfeits / stolen / contraband goods)
- Integration with customs clearance (check automation of the clearance form)
- Warehousing
