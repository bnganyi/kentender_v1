A simple requirement, a clear decision

Usability amendment v0.1 · 13 September 2026
Approved by the Project Owner · 13 September 2026. Approved design basis for incorporation into NDS v1.12.

Retain one requirement, six source fields and one Head of Department review. Make the normal task and each decision’s consequence understandable. No additional approval, funding input or procurement task is introduced.

1. Every actor, one appropriate journey

ActorWork and presentationBoundary retained
Departmental AuthorDescribe own requirement → submit → see the decision. Correct returned work directly; propose changes or request withdrawal after acceptance.Own records only under authorised departmental scope. Accepted changes require review; the original remains in effect meanwhile.

Updated requirements show changes first; withdrawal shows request and current Planning dependency.No decision on own submitted revision or own withdrawal request. Users with several responsibilities see their permitted work in the same workspace. No role switch or extra dashboard. Finance, AO, statutory approvers, suppliers and bidders have no added Departmental Needs task.

2. Keep the form short and the workspace useful

Form heading: Create a departmental need. Introduction: Describe one requirement for your department. Your Head of Department will review it for procurement planning.

LabelGuidanceStored field — unchanged

DescriptionDescribe what is needed.description
Expected resultWhat will the department be able to do when this need is met?expected_operational_result

Use the existing limits, exact quantity precision and governed unit options. Description states the requirement; Expected result states its intended benefit. Do not merge them or add a justification, budget, attachment or classification field.

Department and financial year are supplied when unambiguous. Where the Author has several eligible departments, put Department at the top of this new form instead of a separate “Create need for” modal and Continue step. Require selection before the first write; the owning department remains fixed thereafter. The year still comes from the effectively open intake, never an unrestricted user choice.

Keep Save draft and Submit for review as distinct actions. Submission should not require a prior manual Save click. When implementation needs save followed by submit, it must retain the created Draft if submission fails and report the result accurately; this proposal does not claim those two existing commands are already one atomic operation.

Workspace: names before references; columns Requirement, Quantity, Required by, Status and Action. An accepted requirement may carry a short, clearly labelled annual-plan status where owner information is confirmed. A Draft need does not require a repeated “Not included” Planning badge. HoD pending decisions lead the workspace; the complete departmental register stays available below without a second competing review queue.

Waiting: Awaiting Head of Department review. Returned: Changes requested, with Correct and resubmit opening the existing correction Draft. Example feedback: “Check how many staff need laptops and update the quantity.” Keep the actual reviewer’s full comment and history; never automatically rewrite their evidence.

Closed initial intake: “New submissions are closed. You can save changes to this draft and submit if submissions reopen.” This includes resubmission of an unaccepted initial Need under NDS v1.11. Accepted-Need updates and withdrawal requests follow their own rules. Do not import Planning’s different correction-window exception.

3. Review the requirement, not its version machinery

The HoD sees all six submitted facts on one page, with the requester, department and submission time separately labelled. Exact Need/reference/revision remains secondary. No required download, checklist, score or opening every disclosure.

Decision contextAction labelConsequence beside the action
Initial need — acceptAccept for planningThis makes the requirement available for departmental procurement planning. It does not approve spending or start procurement.
Initial need — declineDo not take forwardThis requirement will not be made available for planning. Explain why.
Initial need or update — correctionReturn for correctionThe requester will receive your comment and can correct and resubmit the requirement.
Accepted need — accept updateAccept proposed changesThese details will replace the previously accepted requirement for future Planning use. Existing departmental and annual plans are not changed automatically.
Accepted need — decline updateDecline proposed changesThe previously accepted requirement will remain in effect. Explain why these changes are declined.

Place the acceptance consequence directly beside the deliberate decision action and remove the repeated generic Accept confirmation dialog. No acceptance reason or new checkbox is added. Return and decline still open their required reason form, labelled What needs to change? and Why are you declining this requirement? or Why are you declining these changes?, respectively. Keep the existing 20–1,000 character rule.

5. Withdrawal: who must act and what remains

The Author requests withdrawal with the existing required reason. The HoD reviews the complete accepted requirement, request and current Planning dependency. No Author shortcut withdraws an accepted Need; open update and withdrawal requests remain mutually exclusive.

The full six-field accepted requirement appears on this review using Sketch A’s labelled detail block. It is omitted from this compact difference sketch only; the implemented review must not omit it.

If confirmed clear: show “This requirement is not included in the current annual plan.” Offer Approve withdrawal / Decline withdrawal. Retain the existing focused withdrawal confirmation and explicit consequence: “This withdraws the accepted requirement. Earlier decisions remain in history.” A reason is still required for decline.

If unavailable: “Planning information could not be checked. Withdrawal cannot be approved until the check succeeds.” A failed read never means no dependency. Approval checks every accepted revision of the stable Need with Planning at commit, including an older version still in use.

The responsible-role message creates no new task, notification or automatic request in Planning. A permitted plan link is navigation only. Planning’s existing scope and downstream-use rules may prevent removal; the screen must not promise that withdrawal will become possible or offer the HoD a way to bypass them.

7. Short verification round before NDS v1.12

Give realistic tasks without explaining button names or internal revisions. Record completion, hesitation, wrong turns, requests for help and the participant’s explanation of the outcome. The amendment and sketches have not been tested with representative users.
