Designed for:

straightforward single-lot procurement of IT equipment such as laptops, desktops, tablets, monitors, printers, scanners, network equipment, power-protection equipment and peripherals; unreserved procurement or the supported reserved treatments above; delivery, installation, configuration, testing or orientation only when ancillary to the Goods.

Not designed for:
