KenTender · Budget & Funding

Record the allocation. Understand what remains.

Usability amendment v0.1 · 13 September 2026
Proposed for Project Owner review.

Budget & Funding needs a usability revamp that makes three things clear: what has been registered, what is proposed to change, and what is already reserved or committed.

Keep the Budget Officer → Budget Approver flow. KenTender records an externally approved allocation. Its approval activates that record for procurement control; it does not approve the public budget or release cash. Finance’s whole-plan review stays in Procurement Planning.

3. Record and update without repeated work

Keep the evidence form small

Use Record approved allocation. The introduction is Enter the allocation approved outside KenTender and attach its approval document. Year and currency are visible; currency comes from the verified owner metadata and is never guessed or editable. Remove the empty “Budget reference: Not assigned” input. Show the generated reference as secondary detail after the first successful save.

Existing valueVisible label / guidancePreserved contract
approval_referenceApproval referenceExternal instrument identifier, not a new internal approval number.
approval_dateApproval dateThe external approval date; future dates fail validation.
authorised_totalApproved allocationPositive total approved by the external instrument, in the fixed currency.
approval_document_file_idApproval documentExactly one current document. Replacement in a Draft preserves every prior submitted attempt’s evidence.
Line titleBudget lineThe funded purpose, with no second description or purpose field.
owner_org_unit_idAvailable toAll departments for null/Entity-wide, or the exact named department. Guidance: Which department may use this budget line? No automatic descendant eligibility.
funding_sourceFunding sourceExisting governed source; no free-text fallback.
approved_amountAmountPositive exact allocation for that line; current approved line identities are not editable in an update.

Retain the useful first-save boundary: Save and add budget lines creates the Budget and Draft once, then opens the lines. The successor must state actual save-time versus submit-time requiredness, retaining permitted Draft saving without adding a second competing save action to this first screen. Do not claim an incomplete attachment or missing data has been submitted. Unknown creation results are resolved using the original request identity, not by creating another Budget.

Names lead line references. Show a compact running total: Approved allocation, Total entered and, when unequal, Amount still to assign or Amount over allocation. A balanced Draft says Budget lines match the approved allocation. The server confirms exact totals; previews cannot round away a difference or change the approved amount to force a match.

Save changes saves the current editing scope, clearly named. Submit for review includes pending valid approval-detail and line edits through the existing save commands before submitting the confirmed exact version. The full successor must specify token sequencing, upload/link outcomes and stable retry identities. If a save succeeds but submission fails: Your changes were saved, but the allocation was not submitted. If the outcome is unknown, say so and resolve the original request. Do not claim separate save commands are one atomic transaction.

Make updates mean what they do

Rename Create revision to Update registered allocation, with The current allocation stays in use until this update is approved. Reuse the one existing successor Draft; an attempted duplicate offers its authorised route. Label Revision type Type of change, keeping Supplementary allocation, Reduction, Transfer and Correction exactly as stored.

Keep existing line identity values read-only. If title, department eligibility or funding source really changes, explain Add a new budget line for the changed purpose, department or funding source. Do not unlock an approved line or expand the source department silently. Line removal must distinguish omitting an eligible line from the proposed version from deleting its history: BUD-BR-020 permits omission only with no remaining reservation or active commitment. Reconcile the blanket removal prohibition in §12.2 with this rule in the successor; it is not permission to delete an approved record.

For a transfer, show Total moved out and Total moved in; they must match and the total allocation stays unchanged. New positive amounts still require the external approval evidence. Do not introduce a generic justification field or an in-KenTender allocation approval request.

4. Show the decision and its evidence together

Initial registration opens Review registered allocation with the submitted total, complete line set and external approval details. A successor opens Review allocation changes with the exact proposed changes first. Preserve the existing tabs as navigable views, but bring the comparison and material issues onto the opening page. No required tab tour, approval checklist, per-line acceptance or repeat confirmation.

Rename Current floor to Reserved + committed, explained once as This amount must remain covered. Rename Headroom to Available after update. When proposed allocation falls below reserved plus committed, display a Shortfall; do not portray a negative amount as spendable money. The submitted line amounts are fixed. These live protection values carry their observation time and are rechecked at approval.

Approval evidence stays on the same page as structured reference/date/total facts and the exact document link. Provide an in-page preview when supported, plus Open approval document; a preview failure must not conceal an unavailable file or substitute a later Draft upload. Do not force a download, require a checkbox proving the file was opened, fabricate document content or relax the one-document requirement.
