## A. Decisions already settled in the documents (not asked again)

1. The Budget Approver is a different actual person from the entry preparer and from every Finance confirmer whose confirmation supplies the submitted funding basis (BUD §4.12 "Actors and evidence", §8.8 item 4; AUTH §4.4A; CM-AC-87). Refusal code `AUTH_SEGREGATION_BLOCKED`.
2. An independently onboarded contract gets one Initial-or-Annual request and entry lineage per contract and target year; later-year funding needs no reopening and no automatic carry-forward (BUD §8.8; CM §20.4A). Tender-derived procurement stays single-year, one lot, KES.
3. A Closed commitment accepts no ordinary adjustment; it is lowered only by a two-person Budget correction; reopening makes no funding call (BUD §§4.11, 8.7; BUD-BR-033/034).
4. Budget makes no accounting read; CM passes validated funding facts and opaque evidence references (BUD25-AC-010). A native invoice or payment alone is never Budget coverage (BUD §8.7).
5. Screens are in scope: BUD-DES-20, -21, -22 and the CM D12-F, D21-F, D21-A, D09-F/D17 variants (BUD §11.22; CM §10.9). API-only does not satisfy acceptance.
