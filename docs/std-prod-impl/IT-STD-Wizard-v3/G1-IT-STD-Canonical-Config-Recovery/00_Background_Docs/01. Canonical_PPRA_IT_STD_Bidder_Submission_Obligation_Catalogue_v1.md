## 1. Purpose

This catalogue identifies the complete set of bidder-facing, bidder-generated, and platform-enforced submission obligations arising from the canonical PPRA Standard Tender Document for Procurement of Information Technology.

It provides the missing controlled layer between:

```text
Approved PPRA IT STD version
and
Tender-specific Bidder Workspace Manifest
```

The catalogue answers:

> What must a bidder provide, declare, confirm, price, evidence, or cause the electronic platform to enforce for a tender issued under the PPRA IT STD?

This catalogue is the authoritative input to the next artifact:

```text
Canonical PPRA IT STD - Bidder Submission Section Blueprint
```

The later blueprint will decide how these obligations are grouped into bidder-facing sections. This catalogue deliberately does not freeze screen names, checklist order, drawer layouts, or implementation components.

## 2. Control Decision

The bidder-submission model must not be derived directly from:

- the NSSF tender document;
- a tender-specific extraction file;
- a generated tender preview;
- PDF page order;
- evaluation-stage headings;
- a manually authored checklist; or
- a Cursor-generated schema.

The correct derivation is:

```text
Approved PPRA IT STD Version
-> Canonical Bidder Submission Obligation Catalogue
-> Canonical IT Bidder Submission Section Blueprint
-> Approved Tender Configuration Snapshot
-> Compiled and Versioned Bidder Workspace Manifest
-> Bidder Response Instances
-> Sealed Bid Submission
```

The NSSF tender may supply configured values, requirements, criteria, evidence demands, price lines, schedules, and contract carry-forward values. It may not supply the master legal form structure or universal checklist taxonomy.

## 3. Source Authority and Precedence

### 3.1 Primary authority

The primary source is the exact approved PPRA IT STD version bound to the tender.

The current source package includes:

- Invitation to Tender;
- Section I - Instructions to Tenderers;
- Section II - Tender Data Sheet;
- Section III - Evaluation and Qualification Criteria;
- Section IV - Tendering Forms;
- Procuring Entity's Requirements;
- Technical Requirements;
- Implementation Schedule;
- System Inventory Tables;
- Background and Informational Materials;
- General Conditions of Contract;
- Special Conditions of Contract; and
- Contract Forms and appendices.

The Form of Tender is the form amended and issued pursuant to PPRA Circular No. 02/2022.

### 3.2 Controlled tender configuration

Tender-specific configuration may resolve only approved configuration slots, including:

- tender identity;
- procuring entity;
- lots;
- dates and deadlines;
- tender validity;
- currencies;
- permitted alternatives;
- security requirements;
- evaluation criteria;
- technical requirements;
- implementation schedule;
- system inventory;
- price schedule lines;
- additional documents expressly permitted by the TDS;
- SCC values; and
- conditional forms and evidence.

Configuration may not:

- shorten a locked legal form;
- remove an applicable statutory declaration;
- replace a structured form with a generic upload;
- create an obligation unsupported by the STD or an approved configurable slot;
- change the meaning of an approved clause;
- redefine evaluation criteria after publication; or
- convert post-award contract forms into bid-preparation requirements.

### 3.3 Calibration fixture

The NSSF SPS ERP tender is a calibration fixture only.

If the NSSF fixture conflicts with, shortens, relocates, or omits canonical content:

1. the bound PPRA IT STD controls;
2. approved tender configuration controls only within permitted slots;
3. the canonical obligation catalogue remains unchanged;
4. the NSSF fixture is corrected or remapped; and
5. the generated bidder workspace is regenerated.

### 3.4 Source precedence

| Priority | Source | Authority |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Bound approved PPRA IT STD version | Legal and structural authority |
| 2 | Approved STD configuration-slot definitions | Defines what a tender instance may configure |
| 3 | Approved and published tender configuration snapshot | Supplies resolved tender-specific values |
| 4 | Approved electronic-submission policy | Supplies governed electronic equivalents for paper mechanics |
| 5 | Generated bidder workspace manifest | Compiled output; cannot redefine upstream rules |
| 6 | Calibration fixture | Test data only |

## 4. Scope and Boundaries

### 4.1 Included

This catalogue includes:

- legal forms completed as part of a bid;
- declarations and certifications;
- bidder identity and ownership disclosures;
- eligibility and qualification responses;
- preliminary evaluation evidence;
- technical proposal obligations;
- item-by-item requirement responses;
- supporting evidence;
- implementation and personnel responses;
- price schedules;
- tender security or tender-securing declarations;
- conditional alternative, lot, JV, foreign-tenderer, manufacturer, and subcontractor obligations;
- electronic equivalents for paper submission mechanics;
- whole-bid validation, authorization, sealing, timestamping, and receipt controls;
- downstream evaluation and contract carry-forward routing; and
- explicit exclusions for post-award forms.

### 4.2 Excluded

This catalogue does not define:

- final bidder checklist section names or order;
- UX layout;
- Stitch prompts;
- component design;
- database table names;
- API routes;
- Cursor implementation instructions;
- actual evaluation decisions;
- bid opening actions;
- award decisions;
- contract administration; or
- project execution after award.

### 4.3 No document-filling model

The electronic bidder workspace is the submission surface.

PDF and rendered tender documents are references and legal reproductions. They are not the primary response mechanism.

The system must not require a bidder to:

- fill a blank PDF;
- upload a signed copy of a form already implemented electronically;
- upload a company letterhead merely to reproduce verified organization identity;
- upload a signature or seal image as the default authorization mechanism; or
- manually duplicate data already owned by another controlled source.

## 6. Canonical Source Anchors

### 6.1 Stable anchor requirement

Every obligation must reference a stable canonical source anchor.

PDF page number is insufficient because pagination may change between:

- source DOCX;
- official PDF;
- corrected PDF;
- accessible render;
- electronic tender preview; and
- future approved versions.

Page numbers may be retained as import provenance, but they may not be the canonical identity.

### 6.2 Anchor convention

| Source area | Anchor pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Instructions to Tenderers | `ITSTD.ITT.<clause>` | `ITSTD.ITT.13.1.a` |
| Tender Data Sheet | `ITSTD.TDS.<ITT-reference>` | `ITSTD.TDS.ITT22.1` |
| Evaluation and Qualification | `ITSTD.SECIII.<item>` | `ITSTD.SECIII.PRELIM` |
| Form of Tender | `ITSTD.SECIV.FOT.<item>` | `ITSTD.SECIV.FOT.l` |
| Confidential Business Questionnaire | `ITSTD.SECIV.CBQ.<item>` | `ITSTD.SECIV.CBQ.e.ii` |
| Declaration | `ITSTD.SECIV.<form-code>` | `ITSTD.SECIV.SD1` |
| Price form | `ITSTD.SECIV.PRICE.<form-code>` | `ITSTD.SECIV.PRICE.RECURRENT_SUMMARY` |
| Qualification form | `ITSTD.SECIV.QUAL.<form-code>` | `ITSTD.SECIV.QUAL.EXP-2` |
| Technical conformity form | `ITSTD.SECIV.TECH.<form-code>` | `ITSTD.SECIV.TECH.RESPONSIVENESS` |
| Procuring Entity requirement | `ITSTD.REQ.<stable-item-id>` | `ITSTD.REQ.FUNCTIONAL.001` |
| Implementation schedule | `ITSTD.IMPL.<stable-item-id>` | `ITSTD.IMPL.MILESTONE.001` |
| System inventory | `ITSTD.INVENTORY.<stable-item-id>` | `ITSTD.INVENTORY.RECURRENT.001` |
| GCC or SCC | `ITSTD.GCC.<clause>` / `ITSTD.SCC.<clause>` | `ITSTD.GCC.13` |
| Contract form | `ITSTD.CONTRACT_FORM.<form-code>` | `ITSTD.CONTRACT_FORM.PERFORMANCE_SECURITY` |

### 6.3 Printed numbering inconsistencies

The source document contains internal numbering inconsistencies in some headings and cross-references.

The STD Engine must:

1. preserve the approved printed legal text;
2. record the printed heading and printed reference;
3. assign a stable internal canonical identifier;
4. maintain an explicit cross-reference map; and
5. prohibit downstream modules from silently correcting or renumbering the legal source.

## 7. Obligation Record Contract

Every canonical obligation record must contain:

| Attribute | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `obligation_id` | Stable identifier unique within the STD version |
| `canonical_title` | Short controlled title |
| `source_anchor` | Stable canonical source reference |
| `printed_source_reference` | Human-readable source clause or form |
| `obligation_class` | Form, declaration, evidence, structured response, price, acknowledgement, platform control, or post-award exclusion |
| `actor` | Bidder organization, authorized representative, third-party issuer, or platform |
| `applicability_rule` | Always, configured, conditional, derived, or post-award |
| `requiredness_rule` | Exact rule determining whether omission blocks submission |
| `fulfilment_model` | Electronic form, derived value, structured rows, evidence, confirmation, or platform enforcement |
| `value_owner` | STD, tender instance, bidder organization, bidder response, or system |
| `dependencies` | Upstream values or related obligations |
| `validation_rule` | Minimum completion and consistency rules |
| `invalidation_triggers` | Changes that make an earlier response or confirmation stale |
| `evaluation_route` | Preliminary, technical, financial, informational, or not evaluated |
| `opening_route` | Opening register disclosure, presence-only check, or confidential until evaluation |
| `contract_route` | No carry-forward, reference, award carry-forward, or post-award task |
| `evidence_policy` | None, optional, required, conditional, or third-party instrument |
| `audit_policy` | Events and versions that must be retained |
| `status` | Draft, validated, approved, active, superseded, or retired |

No obligation may be published without these attributes being resolved.

### 8.1 Obligation classes

| Code | Class | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `ACK` | Acknowledgement | Bidder confirms review of current tender material |
| `FORM` | Structured legal form | Exact governed form implemented electronically |
| `DECL` | Declaration | Legally material confirmation or certification |
| `QUAL` | Qualification response | Structured eligibility, capacity, experience, financial or personnel response |
| `EVID` | Evidence | Supporting file or authoritative evidence record |
| `TECH` | Technical response | Proposal, methodology, compliance or supporting technical response |
| `PRICE` | Commercial response | Price lines, totals, taxes, discounts and currencies |
| `SEC` | Security instrument | Tender security or tender-securing declaration |
| `PLATFORM` | Platform control | Deadline, sealing, authorization, immutability or receipt control |
| `POST_AWARD` | Post-award exclusion | Contract-stage item explicitly excluded from bid preparation |

### 8.2 Applicability

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `ALWAYS` | Applies to every tender under the bound IT STD version |
| `CONFIGURED` | Applies when an approved tender configuration slot enables or populates it |
| `CONDITIONAL` | Applies when bidder or tender circumstances satisfy a rule |
| `DERIVED` | Generated from another canonical or configured record collection |
| `POST_AWARD` | Applies only after an award-stage event |

### 8.3 Fulfilment models

| Model | Use |
|---|---|
| `READ_AND_ACKNOWLEDGE` | Current tender documents and addenda |

| `STRUCTURED_RESPONSE_PLUS_EVIDENCE` | Response fields plus linked evidence |
| `REQUIREMENT_RESPONSE_ROW` | Item-level compliance, explanation, deviation and evidence |

| `THIRD_PARTY_INSTRUMENT` | Security, authorization or certification issued by another party |
| `AUTHORIZED_CONFIRMATION` | Confirmation by a verified authorized representative |
| `PLATFORM_ENFORCEMENT` | Server-enforced deadline, sealing, timestamp or immutability |

### 8.4 Downstream routes

| Route | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `OPENING` | Approved fields may be disclosed in the bid opening register |
| `PRELIMINARY` | Used in responsiveness or mandatory preliminary evaluation |

## 9. Governance and Lifecycle

### 9.1 Catalogue lifecycle

```text
Draft
-> Validated
-> Submitted for Approval
-> Approved
-> Active
-> Superseded or Retired
```

| State | Meaning | Permitted outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Obligations are being extracted and mapped | Edit and review |
| Validated | Coverage, source anchors and internal consistency have passed validation | Submit for approval |
| Submitted for Approval | Controlled approval is pending | Approve or return |
| Approved | Catalogue version is approved but not yet the active version | Activate |
| Active | May be bound to new tender STD instances | Read-only |
| Superseded | Replaced by a newer approved version; existing bindings remain reproducible | Read-only |
| Retired | Not available for new tenders | Read-only |

### 9.2 Roles and permissions

| Capability | STD Template Administrator | Legal / Procurement Reviewer | STD Approver | Tender Configurator | Tender Reviewer | System Compiler |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Draft obligation records | Yes | No | No | No | No | No |
| Assign source anchors | Yes | Review | No | No | No | No |
| Validate legal coverage | Support | Yes | No | No | No | Automated checks |
| Approve catalogue version | No | Recommend | Yes | No | No | No |
| Activate approved version | Controlled | No | Yes | No | No | No |
| Configure tender values | No | No | No | Yes | Review | No |
| Change canonical obligation | Draft only | Review | Approve | No | No | No |
| Compile manifest | No | No | No | No | No | Yes |
| Override active catalogue at runtime | No | No | No | No | No | No |

### 9.3 Publication freeze

When a tender is published, the system must freeze:

- bound STD version;
- catalogue version;
- section blueprint version;
- tender configuration version;
- bidder workspace manifest version;
- locked legal-text versions;
- requirement rows;
- price rows;
- evaluation links;
- applicability decisions; and
- validation-rule versions.

### 9.4 Addenda

An addendum must create a new published tender version and a new bidder workspace manifest version.

The system must compute an impact set identifying:

- affected obligations;
- affected fields or rows;
- affected evidence;
- affected price calculations;
- affected declarations;
- affected authorized confirmations; and
- required bidder re-acknowledgements.

Unaffected responses may carry forward. Materially affected confirmations must be invalidated and reconfirmed.

### 9.5 No downstream redefinition

Bidder Workspace, Bid Opening, Evaluation, Award and Contract modules may consume this catalogue and its compiled outputs.

They may not:

- add a submission obligation;
- remove an obligation;
- change requiredness;
- alter a declaration;
- change an evaluation route;
- change a published price rule; or
- alter contract carry-forward behavior.

## 15. Dependency and Invalidation Rules

| Dependency change | Obligations affected |
|---|---|
| Tender or procuring-entity identity | Form of Tender, CBQ, declarations, security, authority and final confirmation |
| New or amended addendum | Document acknowledgement and every obligation in the addendum impact set |
| Lot selection | Requirements, qualification, implementation, prices and Form of Tender totals |
| Alternatives policy or selected alternative | Alternative package, technical response, prices and Form of Tender |
| Price Schedule change | Price summaries, discounts and Form of Tender confirmation |
| Bidder legal identity change | CBQ, ELI forms, declarations, authority and Form of Tender |
| JV membership change | JV forms, qualification, security, authority and Form of Tender |
| Authorized representative change | All current authorized confirmations |
| Requirement or evidence rule change through addendum | Affected requirement responses, evidence and technical confirmation |
| Evaluation criterion change through addendum | Affected qualification/technical responses and validation |
| Legal-text version change | Every acceptance or declaration referencing that text |
| Tender security configuration change | Security instrument/declaration and Form of Tender |
| Currency, tax or pricing rule change | Price Schedule and Form of Tender |

On invalidation:

1. retain the previous response and confirmation in audit history;
2. mark it superseded or stale;
3. identify the changed dependency;
4. recalculate completion and blocker status;
5. require review or reconfirmation where material; and
6. prevent submission until current mandatory obligations pass.

## 16. Downstream Routing Controls

### 16.1 Bid opening

Only fields expressly approved for opening disclosure may be exposed at bid opening.

The catalogue and later blueprint must distinguish:

- bidder identity;
- lot or alternative identity;
- tender price and permitted discount disclosure;
- tender security presence or other opening particulars required by the governing process;
- submission timestamp; and
- confidential technical, qualification and evidence content.

Bid opening may not redefine which values are disclosed.

### 16.2 Evaluation

Every published evaluation criterion must reference one or more canonical obligation IDs or derived values.

Evaluation may:

- assess a response;
- verify evidence;
- record findings;
- apply published pass/fail rules;
- assign published scores; and
- calculate published financial results.

Evaluation may not request a missing unpublished submission item as though it had been mandatory at closing.

### 16.3 Contract carry-forward

Carry-forward must use the immutable submitted response version and the approved award decision.

Potential carry-forward includes:

- accepted technical offer;
- implementation schedule;
- key personnel commitments where applicable;
- subcontractors and manufacturers;
- software and custom-material classifications;
- accepted price schedules;
- warranties and support commitments;
- deviations expressly accepted through the lawful process; and
- bidder disclosures required for contract preparation.

The Contract module may not alter the submitted source record.

3. every mandatory preliminary criterion has a bidder response or evidence route;
4. every technical criterion links to published bidder-response content;
5. every configured technical requirement has a stable ID and response model;
6. every configured price item belongs to a canonical price table;
7. Form of Tender prices are derived from the Price Schedule;
8. every conditional rule is resolved or remains validly bidder-dependent;
9. every locked declaration references an approved legal-text version;
10. every evidence requirement has type, issuer, validity and linkage rules where applicable;
11. all opening disclosures are predetermined;
12. all contract carry-forward mappings are predetermined;
13. no background narrative contains an unmapped mandatory obligation;
14. no post-award form is incorrectly required during bid preparation;
15. no NSSF fixture value has entered the canonical blueprint; and
16. the complete manifest can be reproduced from the bound versions and configuration snapshot.

## 19. Acceptance Criteria

### 19.1 Canonical completeness

- All ITT 13 bidder-submission components are represented.
- All applicable Section IV forms have explicit electronic dispositions.
- Preliminary, qualification, technical, financial and conditional obligations are distinguishable.
- Physical submission mechanics have approved electronic equivalents or explicit exclusions.
- Post-award forms are not misclassified as bid-preparation tasks.

### 19.2 Legal fidelity

- Locked declarations are referenced from the bound STD version.
- No tender fixture shortens or paraphrases canonical form obligations.
- Printed numbering inconsistencies remain traceable through stable internal anchors.
- The Form of Tender remains complete and dependent on its related declarations and Price Schedule.

### 19.3 Electronic-first behavior

- The bidder completes structured electronic responses.
- A PDF is not the completion surface.
- No signed-form upload substitutes for an electronic form.
- Paper original, copy, serialization, signature-image and envelope mechanics are replaced by governed platform controls.

### 19.4 Dynamic tender behavior

- Requirement, criterion, evidence and price rows may vary by tender without changing the canonical catalogue.
- Lots, alternatives, JV, security, foreign-tenderer, manufacturer and subcontractor obligations are conditionally generated.
- Currency, VAT, validity and tender-specific thresholds are configuration values rather than canonical constants.

### 19.5 Governance

- Catalogue versions follow approval and activation states.
- Published tenders bind immutable versions.
- Addenda generate new versions and impact-based invalidation.
- Downstream modules cannot redefine submission obligations or evaluation links.

### 19.6 Downstream integrity

- Bid Opening consumes only predetermined opening fields.
- Evaluation consumes structured responses linked to published criteria.
- Contract carry-forward references immutable submitted responses.
- Every submission is reproducible from versioned inputs.

## 20. Smoke Contract

The catalogue fails if any of the following is possible:

1. Cursor can use an NSSF-specific schema as the canonical bidder-workspace contract.
2. A canonical obligation contains `NSSF`, `Microsoft`, `KES`, a fixed VAT rate, or another tender-instance value outside an expressly labelled calibration appendix.
3. An ITT 13 submission component has no obligation mapping.
4. A Section IV form has no electronic or post-award disposition.
5. A PDF page number is the only source identity.
6. A bidder must upload a signed Form of Tender instead of completing the governed electronic form.
7. A bidder enters a Form of Tender price separately from the Price Schedule.
8. The abbreviated NSSF CBQ replaces the canonical PPRA CBQ.
9. A statutory declaration is reduced to an unlabeled generic checkbox.
10. A form is duplicated as both an electronic response and a mandatory upload.
11. Background narrative creates a hidden bidder obligation.
12. A tender-specific preliminary criterion is treated as a permanent canonical form field.
13. A generic Contract Conditions Acknowledgement is generated without an explicit canonical basis.
14. Submit and Seal is represented as a normal section field.
15. A post-award performance security or beneficial-ownership form is required before bid submission without legal basis.
16. A bidder workspace manifest can be published without section type, requiredness, applicability, dependencies, completion rules and version bindings.
17. An addendum can materially change an obligation without invalidating affected acknowledgements or confirmations.
18. Evaluation can request an unpublished mandatory item after bid closing.
19. A downstream module changes a published obligation, criterion, disclosure or contract route.
20. The sealed bid cannot be reconstructed exactly from retained versions and audit records.
