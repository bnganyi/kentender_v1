**Recovery path**

- **Freeze the existing NSSF output**
  - Do not delete it.
  - Mark 10_NSSF... as deprecated and fixture-only.
  - Rename its eventual role to something like NSSF_Bidder_Workspace_Manifest_Golden.json.
- **Create a canonical IT STD obligation catalogue**
  - Audit ITT 13-18, Section III, Section IV and relevant Sections V-IX.
  - Record every bidder obligation as:
    - mandatory, conditional or post-award;
    - electronic form, structured response, evidence, acknowledgement or price response;
    - source legal-text reference;
    - configuration owner;
    - evaluation destination;
    - invalidation dependencies.
- **Create the canonical IT Bidder Submission Blueprint**
  - Define stable semantic section keys and renderer types.
  - Preserve exact form wording through locked legal-text references.
  - Define conditional rules for lots, JV, alternatives, security, foreign bidders and subcontracting.
  - Keep checklist grouping separate from legal document numbering.
- **Create a strict manifest contract and compiler**
  - The compiler should perform:
  - canonical blueprint
  - \+ approved tender configuration snapshot
  - \+ electronic-submission platform policy

→ published bidder workspace manifest

- - It must never compile directly from an NSSF extraction file.

- **Remap NSSF**
  - Retain the useful data:
    - 190 requirements;
    - 22 price lines;
    - preliminary criteria;
    - qualification criteria;
    - scoring;
    - schedules;
    - SCC carry-forward values.
  - Bind those records to canonical configuration slots and obligation IDs.
  - Microsoft-specific requirements remain tender-instance data and must not modify canonical PPRA forms.
- **Regenerate and migrate**
  - Generate a new NSSF manifest from the canonical blueprint.
  - Map old section keys to new keys.
  - Invalidate old confirmations where the legal form or dependencies changed.
  - Preserve earlier responses and audit history where mappings are unambiguous.
- **Strengthen acceptance tests**
  - Every ITT 13 bidder document is accounted for.
  - Every applicable Section IV form is represented.
  - No NSSF, Microsoft, KES or fixed VAT appears in the canonical blueprint.
  - A second, materially different IT tender produces a different manifest without code changes.
  - No signed-form upload duplicates an electronic form.
  - Addenda invalidate affected acknowledgements and confirmations.
  - Review, Submit & Seal and Receipt remain workflow gates.

The universal Bidder Workspace shell and dynamic renderers remain sound. The recovery should therefore focus on the manifest-production pipeline, not restart the interface.
