## 1. Purpose

This specification defines how KenTender shall render, complete, validate, confirm and audit the Form of Tender as a structured electronic bidder response.

The Form of Tender is not:

- a downloadable blank form;
- a PDF-filling task;
- a signed-document upload;
- an independent price-entry surface; or
- a generic declaration assembled by the Bidder Workspace.

It is a governed electronic projection of the exact Form of Tender contained in the published tender's bound Standard Tender Document version, resolved with tender-specific configuration and bidder-specific response data.

The user outcome is:

> Review the tender particulars and derived offer, complete the required disclosures, accept the applicable legal declarations, and have an authorized representative confirm the Form of Tender electronically.

## 2. Source Authority and Precedence

### 2.1 Governing source

The legal source for the current proof of concept is:

- `DOC 10. STD FOR PROCUREMENT OF INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY.pdf`;
- Section IV - Tendering Forms;
- Form of Tender;
- amended and issued pursuant to PPRA Circular No. 02/2022;
- printed pages 35-37, corresponding to PDF pages 51-53.

The official PPRA STD version bound to the published tender remains authoritative. The electronic form schema must retain traceability to that exact version and its clause structure.

### 2.2 Tender-instance validation source

The NSSF SPS ERP tender is used only as a configured tender-instance fixture:

- Tender reference: `NSSFSPS/ICT/ERP/001/2025-2026`;
- Procuring entity: National Social Security Fund Staff Pension Scheme;
- procurement category: Information Technology / ERP System;
- currency: KES;
- tender validity: 154 days;
- alternatives: not permitted;
- configured price summary: total excluding VAT, VAT at 16%, and grand total including VAT.

The NSSF Form 1 is not the legal master. It is substantially shorter than the PPRA Form of Tender and does not contain the complete PPRA declaration set.

### 2.3 Precedence rule

If the NSSF fixture, mapping pack or generated schema conflicts with or omits content from the bound PPRA STD:

1. the bound PPRA STD controls;
2. approved tender configuration controls only within the STD's configurable slots;
3. the published bidder workspace manifest reflects the resolved legal structure; and
4. the fixture must be corrected rather than the legal form being reduced.

## 4. Workspace Position and Ownership

### 4.1 Checklist position

For the current IT tender fixture:

```text
Submission Checklist
-> 2. Form of Tender
-> Electronic Form of Tender screen
```

The displayed ordinal is not the section identity. The section is resolved using its manifest identity.

Recommended manifest classification:

| Attribute | Value |
|---|---|
| Section key | `form_of_tender` |
| Section type | `declaration_form` |
| Display label | From the published manifest; currently `Form of Tender` |
| Required | True for the current PPRA IT STD |
| Blocks submission | True |

### 4.2 This section owns

- display of the resolved legal Form of Tender;
- bidder-controlled disclosures required by that form;
- explicit acceptance of applicable declarations;
- derived offer and price summary;
- signatory confirmation;
- cross-section dependency validation;
- form-level audit history; and
- completion status reported to the Submission Checklist.

### 4.3 This section does not own

- tender identity or configuration;
- price-line entry or calculation;
- lot selection where owned by another section;
- addendum issuance or acknowledgement;
- organization registration data;
- authorized-representative administration;
- eligibility evaluation;
- bid submission and sealing; or
- document generation as the authoritative response.

### 5.1 Non-linear checklist rule

The Form of Tender may be opened before the Price Schedule and related declarations are complete. It must then display unresolved dependencies and remain `In Progress` or `Needs Attention`.

Checklist order must not cause duplicate manual entry. The section becomes `Complete` only after its current dependencies pass validation and an authorized representative confirms the current form version.

## 6. Electronic Interaction Model

The Form of Tender screen contains four task groups.

### 6.1 Tender and bidder details

Shows the parties and tender being confirmed. Values are read-only unless the user follows a governed link to correct bidder organization data.

### 6.2 Offer and price summary

Shows the configured offer description, applicable lots, currencies, price totals, discounts and tender validity. Commercial values are derived from their owning sections.

### 6.3 Declarations and disclosures

Shows the applicable PPRA declarations as structured confirmations and renders the limited disclosure inputs required by the form.

### 6.4 Authorized confirmation

Shows the authorized representative, confirmation statement and confirmation timestamp. Only an authorized role can complete this task.

The screen must always answer:

```text
What remains before this Form of Tender is complete?
```

The bidder must choose one:

- `None paid or payable`; or
- `Disclose commissions, gratuities or fees`.

| Recipient address | Structured address or text | Yes |
| Reason | Text | Yes |
| Amount | Money | Yes |
| Currency | Controlled currency | Yes |

Display read-only completion cards for:

- Confidential Business Questionnaire;
- Certificate of Independent Tender Determination;
- Fraud and Corruption Self-Declaration; and
- Code of Ethical Conduct declaration.

Each card shows:

- status;
- outstanding issue count; and
- `Complete` or `Review` action linking to the owning electronic section.

The Form of Tender must not ask the bidder to upload signed copies of these forms.

### 7.7 Authorized representative

| Field | Control | Ownership | Editability |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bidder organization | Read-only | Verified organization | No |
| Authorized representative | Read-only current user or controlled authorized-person selection | Bidder account governance | Conditional |
| Representative title/capacity | Read-only verified role/capacity | Bidder account governance | No during confirmation |
| Confirmation statement | Checkbox or explicit confirm action | Form response | Authorized representative only |
| Confirmation date/time | System timestamp | Confirmation service | No |
| Confirmation event reference | Internal immutable reference | Audit service | No |

- a scanned signature;
- a company seal image;
- an uploaded signed Form of Tender; or

## 9. Validation and Completion Rules

### 9.1 Blocking validations

The Form of Tender cannot be `Complete` if any of the following is true:

1. tender identity or procuring entity is unresolved;
2. bidder legal identity is incomplete or unverified;
3. required addenda remain unacknowledged;
4. the Price Schedule is incomplete or invalid;
5. form totals do not reconcile with the Price Schedule;
6. a required lot price is absent;
7. a discount is declared without complete details or methodology;
8. tender validity is unresolved or unacknowledged;
9. a required legal declaration is not accepted;
10. state-owned status is unanswered or inconsistent;
11. commission disclosure choice is unanswered;
12. any disclosed commission row is incomplete;
13. a required associated form is incomplete;
14. the current user lacks authorized-representative authority;
15. authorized confirmation has not occurred;
16. the confirmation is stale because a dependency changed; or
17. the submission deadline has passed and the workspace is no longer editable.

### 9.2 Warnings

Warnings may include:

- bidder organization data changed after draft preparation but before confirmation;
- optional associated information remains incomplete;
- the signatory's authorization is approaching expiry, where such expiry is modeled; or
- a non-blocking commercial note requires review.

Warnings do not override a blocker.

### 9.3 Derived completion

Section status is calculated:

| Status | Rule |
|---|---|
| Not Started | No bidder-controlled value or confirmation has been saved |
| In Progress | Work has started and no current blocking result requires special attention |
| Needs Attention | At least one blocking validation or stale confirmation exists |
| Complete | All blocking validations pass and current authorized confirmation exists |
| Not Applicable | Allowed only if the bound STD manifest marks the section inapplicable |

Users cannot set status manually.

## 10. Confirmation Invalidation Rules

An existing confirmation becomes stale when any legally material input changes, including:

- an addendum affecting the Form of Tender;
- tender identity, scope, lot or commercial configuration;
- Price Schedule totals, currencies, tax or discounts;
- bidder legal identity;
- authorized representative or authority status;
- state-owned status;
- commissions disclosure;
- any accepted legal declaration text;
- completion or content status of a required associated declaration; or
- the bound STD or published tender version.

On invalidation:

1. retain the prior confirmation in the audit history;
2. mark it `Superseded` or equivalent internally;
3. change the section projection to `Needs Attention`;
4. identify the changed dependency;
5. require the authorized representative to review and reconfirm; and
6. prevent final bid submission until reconfirmed.

No material change may silently retain an earlier confirmation.

## 11. Governance and State Transitions

| From | Event | Guard | To | Actor |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Not Started | First bidder-controlled save | Workspace editable | In Progress | Bidder Admin / Bid Preparer |
| In Progress | Validation detects blocker | Current validation result contains blocker | Needs Attention | System |
| Needs Attention | Blockers resolved without final confirmation | All non-confirmation blockers cleared | In Progress | System |
| In Progress / Needs Attention | Confirm Form of Tender | All blockers cleared; actor authorized; current version displayed | Complete | Authorized Submitter |
| Complete | Material dependency changes | Invalidation rule triggered | Needs Attention | System |
| Complete | Bid submitted and sealed | Whole-bid validation passes | Complete in immutable submitted snapshot | Submission service |

Confirmation is not the same event as whole-bid submission. Submission revalidates the confirmed form and binds it into the sealed bid snapshot.

## 13. Screen Functional Specification

### 13.1 Header

Display:

- manifest section title;
- concise instruction;
- section status;
- issue count;
- last saved time; and
- last confirmed time and representative, when confirmed.

Recommended instruction:

> Review the tender particulars and derived offer, complete the required disclosures, and confirm the Form of Tender through an authorized representative.

### 13.2 Task groups

Display in this order:

1. Tender and Bidder Details
2. Offer and Price Summary
3. Declarations and Disclosures
4. Associated Forms
5. Authorized Confirmation

Each group shows `Complete`, `In Progress` or `Needs Attention`.

### 13.3 Price dependency behavior

If the Price Schedule is incomplete:

- show `Price Schedule incomplete`;
- do not render editable duplicate price fields;
- provide `Complete Price Schedule`; and
- retain the Form of Tender as `In Progress` or `Needs Attention`.

If complete:

- show totals by lot where applicable;
- show discounts, taxes and grand total;
- show amounts in figures and words; and
- provide `Review Price Schedule`.

### 13.4 Declaration behavior

Each applicable declaration shows:

- exact locked legal text or an exact legally approved electronic formulation;
- required confirmation control;
- source-clause trace retained internally; and
- validation message when unanswered.

The default interface must not expose internal clause hashes, schema identifiers or STD version hashes.

### 13.5 Signatory behavior

If the user is not authorized:

- show the selected/eligible authorized representative where available;
- show `Awaiting authorized confirmation`; and
- do not render an enabled confirmation action.

If authorized:

- show the representative's verified name and capacity;
- show the exact confirmation statement;
- require deliberate confirmation; and
- create a timestamped confirmation event.

### 13.6 Actions

| Condition | Primary action | Secondary actions |
|---|---|---|
| Draft dependencies incomplete | Resolve Next Issue | Save Section, Back to Workspace |
| Prepared but awaiting signatory | Send/Leave for Authorized Confirmation, if collaboration supports it | Save Section, Back to Workspace |
| Authorized and valid | Confirm Form of Tender | Save Section, Back to Workspace |
| Complete | Review Confirmation | Back to Workspace, Save & Continue |
| Stale after change | Review Changes and Reconfirm | Back to Workspace |

`Save & Continue` follows manifest order. It does not bypass unresolved blockers or imply that the section is complete.

## 14. Error and Edge-Case Requirements

| Case | Required behavior |
|---|---|
| Price Schedule changes while screen is open | Refresh validation before confirmation; prevent confirmation of stale totals |
| Two preparers edit disclosures | Apply approved concurrency controls; never silently overwrite |
| Signatory authority revoked | Disable confirmation and invalidate an unsubmitted current confirmation |
| Addendum issued | Identify affected content, invalidate confirmation where material, require acknowledgement and reconfirmation |
| Multiple currencies | Show separate currency totals; do not perform unauthorized conversion |
| Multiple lots | Show per-lot and aggregate totals according to configured tender rules |
| Alternatives prohibited | Hide alternative reference and reject alternative-response payload |
| No commissions | Persist explicit `None`, not a blank table |
| Commission disclosed | Require complete structured row and currency |
| State-owned bidder | Require applicable eligibility affirmation |
| Associated declaration incomplete | Show blocker with direct navigation to the owning section |
| Deadline passes during editing | Preserve draft, block further changes and confirmation, show closed state |
| Previously complete form becomes stale | Preserve history and show exact reason for reconfirmation |

## 16. Service and Transaction Requirements

The existing Bidder Workspace service boundary must provide operations equivalent to:

1. retrieve the resolved Form of Tender section;
2. save bidder-controlled disclosures and declaration responses;
3. validate the current section and dependencies;
4. retrieve the current derived price summary;
5. confirm the current form version through an authorized representative;
6. invalidate confirmation when a material dependency changes;
7. report section status, issues and last-updated time to the Submission Checklist; and
8. retrieve confirmation history for authorized users.

### 16.1 Confirmation transaction

Confirmation must be atomic:

1. reload the latest published tender and manifest version;
2. reload current Price Schedule and dependency versions;
3. verify all required addenda acknowledgements;
4. re-run blocking validation;
5. verify current actor authorization;
6. create the immutable rendered-data/version digest;
7. create the confirmation event;
8. set the current confirmation reference; and
9. update the checklist projection.

If any step fails, no current confirmation is created.

### 16.2 Submission transaction

Whole-bid submission must revalidate:

- the confirmation remains current;
- all bound dependency versions are unchanged;
- the actor is authorized to submit; and
- the deadline has not passed.

The submitted snapshot includes the resolved legal text, responses, disclosures, signatory evidence and exact derived price version.

## 17. Audit and Evidentiary Requirements

Record:

- first opening of the section;
- every bidder-controlled save;
- before/after values for disclosures;
- declaration acceptance and withdrawal;
- validation results;
- dependency version changes;
- confirmation creation;
- confirmation invalidation and reason;
- signatory identity and authority snapshot;
- addendum-driven reconfirmation;
- checklist status changes; and
- binding into the submitted bid snapshot.

Audit events must not expose confidential financial values to actors without permission.

The system must be able to reconstruct:

> The exact legal Form of Tender text, tender configuration, derived prices, bidder responses, associated-form statuses and signatory authority that existed at confirmation and at submission.

## 18. Security and Integrity Requirements

1. Only authenticated members of the bidder organization may view the draft form.
2. Financial values must respect bidder-side financial permissions.
3. Only an independently authorized representative may confirm.
4. Confirmation requires deliberate action and approved reauthentication where configured.
5. Confirmation binds an immutable digest of all material form data and dependencies.
6. Any post-confirmation material change invalidates the current confirmation.
7. Submitted form data is immutable and confidential until lawful opening.
8. No procuring-entity user may edit or complete a bidder's Form of Tender.
9. No client-supplied total may override the authoritative Price Schedule calculation.
10. The server is authoritative for timestamps, authorization and validation.

---

## 19. Acceptance Criteria

### 19.1 Source fidelity

- The electronic form contains every applicable declaration from the bound PPRA Form of Tender.
- Locked legal text is not reconstructed from the NSSF fixture.
- Tender configuration only affects permitted parameters and conditional provisions.

### 19.2 Electronic-first behavior

- No blank Form of Tender PDF is used as the completion surface.
- No uploaded signed Form of Tender is required.
- No letterhead, signature image or seal image is required.
- Associated declarations are structured electronic responses.

### 19.3 Data ownership

- Tender identity is prefilled.
- Bidder identity is derived from the verified organization.
- Price values are derived from the Price Schedule.
- Signatory identity is derived from bidder-account authorization.
- The section does not permit duplicate manual price entry.

### 19.4 Validation

- Missing acknowledgements, disclosures, dependencies or authorized confirmation create blockers.
- All values shown in the Form of Tender reconcile with their owning sources.
- Material changes invalidate confirmation.
- Checklist status and issue counts update after saves and confirmation.

### 19.5 Submission integrity

- The submitted form is bound to the exact STD, tender, addendum, Price Schedule and confirmation versions.
- The form is reproducible from structured data.
- The audit trail identifies who confirmed it, under what authority and when.

## 20. Smoke Contract

The implementation fails its smoke contract if any of the following is possible:

1. A bidder manually enters a Form of Tender total that differs from the Price Schedule.
2. A shortened NSSF form replaces the full applicable PPRA Form of Tender.
3. A signed Form of Tender upload is accepted as a substitute for the electronic form.
4. An unauthorized preparer confirms the form.
5. A required addendum is unacknowledged when the form is confirmed.
6. A required associated declaration is incomplete when the form is confirmed.
7. A material price or declaration change leaves the previous confirmation current.
8. A blank commission table is treated as an explicit `None`.
9. A submitted form cannot be reconstructed exactly.
10. A downstream module changes the submitted form.

### 21.1 NSSF bidder-submission schema

The current seven-field `form_of_tender` schema is insufficient. It must be expanded to include:

- all applicable PPRA declarations;
- conditional alternative, security and lot behavior;
- structured commissions disclosure;
- state-owned status;
- cross-section dependency statuses;
- derived price-version binding; and
- governed authorized confirmation.

### 21.2 Preliminary-document mapping

Remove these paper-oriented requirements:

- upload a signed Form of Tender as preliminary evidence;
- upload a completed Certificate of Independent Tender Determination when a structured e-declaration exists; and
- upload a completed Fraud and Corruption Self-Declaration when a structured e-declaration exists.

The preliminary stage should validate completion of the authoritative electronic sections, not duplicate them as files.

### 21.3 Final Declaration and Submission

The final submission section must not recreate the Form of Tender. It must:

- show the current Form of Tender confirmation status;
- revalidate that it remains current;
- require final whole-bid submission confirmation; and
- bind the confirmed form into the sealed submission.
