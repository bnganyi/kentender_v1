**Recommended simplified budget workflow**

| **Step** | **Actor** | **Action** | **Result** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1   | Budget Officer | Create budget and budget lines | Draft budget exists |
| 2   | Budget Officer | Link budget lines to strategy/programmes/objectives | Strategic alignment captured |
| 3   | Budget Officer | Submit budget | Status: Submitted |
| 4   | Budget Owner / Finance Reviewer | Review completeness, amounts, classifications, funding sources, and strategy alignment | Approved forward or returned |
| 5   | Budget Authority | Approve and activate budget | Status: Active |
| 6   | System | Lock approved baseline and create audit record | Budget can fund demands |

**Cleaner status model**

Draft  
→ Submitted  
→ Returned / Approved  
→ Active  
→ Closed

You can collapse **Approved** and **Active** unless you truly need a delay between approval and use.

For simplicity:

Budget Authority approves = budget becomes active

**Role simplification**

| **Old Role** | **Recommended Treatment** |
| --- | --- |
| Budget Officer | Keep |
| Budget Owner | Merge with reviewer role, or keep as the reviewer |
| Finance Reviewer | Merge into Budget Owner / Finance Review role |
| Strategy / Planning Reviewer | Remove as mandatory step; make strategy alignment a validation/check |
| Approval Authority | Keep as Budget Authority |
| Finance / Budget Authority | Merge with Approval Authority |

**Recommended final roles**

Budget Officer  
Budget Reviewer  
Budget Authority  
System

**Strategy alignment should be a check, not another approval layer**

Instead of a separate Strategy/Planning Reviewer, show this during review:

Strategy alignment: Complete  
Linked objectives: 3  
Unlinked budget lines: 0

If there are unlinked lines:

Strategy alignment incomplete.  
Resolve before approval.

Only escalate to Strategy/Planning Reviewer if there is an exception or override.

**Final recommendation**

Use this as the standard flow:

Budget Officer drafts  
→ Budget Reviewer reviews  
→ Budget Authority approves and activates  
→ System locks baseline

Add optional extra reviewers only through configuration for high-value, donor-funded, or exceptional budgets. This keeps the product usable while preserving governance
