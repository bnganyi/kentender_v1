Screen name:

Form Schema Manager

User:

STD Administrator, Forms Designer

Purpose:

Manage tenderer, procuring entity, evaluation, and contract form schemas.

Primary user goals:

\- See all forms defined by the STD.

\- Understand who fills each form and when.

\- Inspect required fields and evidence requirements.

\- Confirm form activation rules.

Required sections:

1\. Form catalog summary.

2\. Search/filter bar.

3\. Forms table.

4\. Form category tabs.

5\. Validation issues panel.

Form categories:

\- Procuring Entity forms

\- Tenderer submission forms

\- Qualification forms

\- Evaluation forms

\- Contract forms

\- Disclosure forms

\- Securities/guarantees

\- Acceptance/change order forms

Required table columns:

\- Form code

\- Form name

\- Respondent

\- Workflow stage

\- Required/conditional

\- Field count

\- Evidence count

\- Activation rules

\- Source anchor

\- Status

\- Actions

Required actions:

\- Open Form

\- Preview Form

\- View Fields

\- View Evidence Requirements

\- View Source

\- Export Schema

Design constraints:

\- Forms are structured schemas, not static attachments.

\- Make respondent and workflow stage explicit.
