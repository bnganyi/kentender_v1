Screen name:

Review and Approval

User:

Internal Reviewer, Legal Reviewer, Procurement Reviewer, Approver

Purpose:

Manage formal review and approval workflow for STD versions.

Primary user goals:

\- Review assigned version.

\- See approval stage and required reviewers.

\- Approve, reject, or request changes.

\- Review validation and source traceability before decision.

\- Preserve decision audit trail.

Required sections:

1\. Approval header with version identity and lifecycle state.

2\. Review stage timeline.

3\. Reviewer checklist.

4\. Validation snapshot.

5\. Source traceability snapshot.

6\. Decision panel.

7\. Review comments/history.

Required fields:

\- Review stage

\- Assigned reviewer

\- Reviewer role

\- Due date

\- Decision

\- Decision reason

\- Conditions

\- Timestamp

\- Digital approval reference

\- Validation snapshot hash

Required actions:

\- Approve

\- Reject

\- Request Changes

\- Add Comment

\- View Validation Report

\- View Source Traceability

\- Return to Structuring

\- Submit to Next Review Stage

Design constraints:

\- Approval must feel formal and auditable.

\- Decision reason should be mandatory for rejection/request changes.
