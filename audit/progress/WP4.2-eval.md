## Follow-ups
1. **AUD-EVL-016 (owner decision, blocked).** EVL §3 requires declaration and confidentiality acceptance of each *member*; it says nothing for the secretary, who reads all bids and prepares the report. Options: (a) leave as is (secretary role grants bid access without declaration); (b) require the secretary to make the same declaration and confidentiality acceptance before any bid read, and refuse bid access to a secretary with a declared conflict (replacement by the Head of Procurement); (c) as (b) plus a conflicted secretary is replaced through a reasoned re-assignment recorded in history. Recommended default: (b)/(c). Nothing implemented. (AUD-EVL-010 already stops a *member*-secretary's access when ineligible.)
2. **Secretary write commands for an ineligible member-secretary (AUD-EVL-010 residue).** `save_narrative`, `send_for_signing`, `retry_delivery` and the clarification send check only `roster.secretary(case) == user`. A member-secretary who declared a conflict loses reads but can still organise/freeze. Recommended default: block those commands for a secretary who is also a member and not eligible. Not done because it widens scope beyond the finding; a one-line guard each if approved.
3. **Two Evaluation tests fail on the test site independent of this work** (see Commands run): test_evl_intake::test_a_final_no_bids_opening_closes_the_preparation (foreign tender TND-MOH-2026-010 in the AO's task list) and test_evl_meetings::test_a_reader_with_no_responsibility_and_no_row_gets_the_forbidden_verdict_and_a_department_head_does_not. Also, mid-session, `tenders/tests/fixtures.py restore_site` hit the command-write guard (User Responsibility Assignment "never deleted directly"); it was clean in the final runs, but whoever owns the guard rollout should confirm test teardown still purges.
