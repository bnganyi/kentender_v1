Same root as Wave 5 AUD-XC-106, XC-121 and PLN-020; PLN v1.29 lines 454, 989, 1244 need the document amendment (D-2).
2. **12-integral-digit storage limit also binds Planning.** Frappe Currency/Float columns are decimal(21,9) (12 integral digits); Planning's `money.parse_money` still allows 18 (`MAX_INTEGRAL_DIGITS`) and Budget enforces 12 pending the owner's storage-type decision (AUD-XC-129, Blocked, lane 3). A Planning amount of 13 to 18 integral digits therefore passes the typed check and fails as a database error. Options: cap Planning at 12 now (matches Budget; one constant, touches `test_money` and `plan_requisition`), or wait for the XC-129 decision. Recommended: cap at 12 when XC-129 is decided. What I did: left 18.
3. **A DPP amount now needs the Fiscal Year's Budget currency.** `_exact_amount` reads the precision through `budget_gateway.money_precision`, so saving an indicative amount for a year with no Budget fails with `PLN_REFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE` before `PLN_BUDGET_LINE_INELIGIBLE`. Correct in production (no Budget means no eligible line), but `test_first_submission_outside_window_is_refused` used a closed year with patched lines and no Budget, so it now patches `money_precision`. Recommended default: keep.
4. **Plan Entry / Plan Source Allocation quantity precision changed 2 to 3** (Needs carry 3 decimals; the copy used to round an accepted 1.005 to 1.00 silently). Field metadata only, no data change; needs the migrate below. Say if the owner wants 2.
