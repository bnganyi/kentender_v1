## Follow-ups

1. **RG-01 / AUD-XC-106: closed by owner decision (7 Oct 2026, Head of Procurement Function).** Left open, by design: a Head who also signed the plan (Plan Preparation Signature) may publish it (no segregation rule exists for publishing; ask if one is wanted); a failed or unknown result is not retried by the Head, it stays the technical retry/reconcile route (PLN section 7.2), so a plan can wait on a technical operator. The Wave 5 rows XC-121 (`PLN_PUBLICATION_FAILED` / `UNKNOWN` are never produced) and PLN-020 (technical operator "Your turn") are separate and not done here.
2. **12-integral-digit storage limit also binds Planning.** Frappe Currency/Float columns are decimal(21,9) (12 integral digits); Planning's `money.parse_money` still allows 18 (`MAX_INTEGRAL_DIGITS`) and Budget enforces 12 pending the owner's storage-type decision (AUD-XC-129, Blocked, lane 3). A Planning amount of 13 to 18 integral digits therefore passes the typed check and fails as a database error. Options: cap Planning at 12 now (matches Budget; one constant, touches `test_money` and `plan_requisition`), or wait for the XC-129 decision. Recommended: cap at 12 when XC-129 is decided. What I did: left 18.
3. **A DPP amount now needs the Fiscal Year's Budget currency.** `_exact_amount` reads the precision through `budget_gateway.money_precision`, so saving an indicative amount for a year with no Budget fails with `PLN_REFERENCE_UNAVAILABLE` before `PLN_BUDGET_LINE_INELIGIBLE`. Correct in production (no Budget means no eligible line), but `test_first_submission_outside_window_is_refused` used a closed year with patched lines and no Budget, so it now patches `money_precision`. Recommended default: keep.
4. **Plan Entry / Plan Source Allocation quantity precision changed 2 to 3** (Needs carry 3 decimals; the copy used to round an accepted 1.005 to 1.00 silently). Field metadata only, no data change; needs the migrate below. Say if the owner wants 2.
5. **`Annual Plan Publication Destination` is not command-only.** It is site configuration; a System Manager can still set `sandbox_outcome` to Fail/Indeterminate. Harmless while the adapter is a sandbox; revisit with a real adapter.
6. **RG-36 (core walk over every app's doctypes) is lane 2.** The Planning module has its own walk now (`TestEveryPlanningRecordIsCommandOnly`); a core test could generalise it to the five core apps with a named allow-list.
7. **Pre-existing failures seen on the test site, none touched by this lane** (same failures named in `WP3.6-needs-planning.md` Follow-ups 6 and INT-B E-4): Planning `test_analytics_provider::test_a_departmental_author_gets_departmental_plans_only_and_no_plan_items`, `test_dead_end_matrix::test_every_annual_plan_state_leaves_every_reader_somewhere_to_go`, `test_home_provider::test_an_accepted_plan_missing_a_later_need_asks_the_department_to_update_it`; Needs `test_departmental_needs_architecture` (2), `test_departmental_needs_seed::test_the_cleared_variant_supplies_no_plan_references`, `test_departmental_needs_lifecycle::test_selectable_years_and_workspace_filtering_span_two_fiscal_years` (ERPNext fiscal-year overlap).
