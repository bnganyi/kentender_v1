## Follow-ups
1. AUD-STR-006 (decision needed, owner). STR never defines "author". Options: (a) submitter only (today); (b) submitter AND any user who saved a Draft edit of this version (events "Draft saved", "Draft structure saved", "Successor Version Created" in the audit trail); (c) only the user who created the version. Recommended default: (b). It is a small change in `strategy_authorization._blocked_by_self_approval` (read the version's Audit Events for those event types plus "Submit for approval") and also applies to Return. Not done because it decides policy and changes who may approve (a single-person Strategy office would be blocked after any edit).
